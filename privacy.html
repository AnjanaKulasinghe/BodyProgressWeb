Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. Your data belongs to you, always.

We never sell, share, or use your personal data for anything outside the Body Progress app. Your personal stats and photos are visible only to you, private by default and encrypted in transit and at rest. The only exception is when you specifically share them using the Fitness Buddy feature or register under your own personal trainer/Coach and expressly grant permission. You can remove that access at any time, your data is 100% secured and in your control.

No Third-Party Sharing

Your data stays with you. We never share with advertisers or data brokers.

Encrypted & Secure

All your photos and health data are encrypted in transit and at rest, with private-by-default storage.

You Control Sharing

Sharing is disabled by default. You choose what and with whom.

Visible Only to You

Your stats and photos are yours alone, unless you specifically share them with a Fitness Buddy or your registered Coach, and you can revoke that access anytime.

What Data We Collect

Body Progress collects only the information necessary to provide you with a great progress tracking experience:

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication details when you sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Progress Photos: Photos you upload to track your transformation journey.
  • Body Measurements: Weight, body fat percentage, muscle mass, and custom measurements you choose to track.
  • Health Data: Optional health metrics if you choose to sync with Apple Health or Google Fit.
  • Usage Data: Basic app analytics to improve performance and fix bugs (anonymous and aggregated).

We only use your email and name to personalize the app and, very occasionally, to send you important app updates. Everything else, your photos, measurements, and health data, stays private to you.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to power the Body Progress experience, tracking, analytics, and your AI-powered self-coaching:

  • Display your progress photos and measurements timeline
  • Generate charts and analytics about your transformation
  • Provide photo comparison tools
  • Power your AI self-coach, workout planning, and personalized insights
  • Send reminder notifications (if enabled by you)
  • Sync your data across your devices via iCloud or Firebase
  • Improve app performance and fix technical issues

We never:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Use your photos or measurements for marketing or advertising
  • Share your information with advertisers or data brokers
  • Use your personal data to train AI models
  • Access your data for any purpose other than providing app functionality

Data Storage & Security

Your privacy and security are paramount:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption.
  • Secure Storage: Photos and measurements are stored securely using Firebase Storage and Cloud Firestore.
  • Authentication: We use secure sign-in with Apple and Google, so we never store your passwords.
  • Private by Default: Your account and all data are private by default. Nothing is public unless you explicitly choose to share.
  • Regular Backups: Your data is automatically backed up to prevent loss, but only you can access it.

Fitness Buddy & Coach Sharing (Optional)

Body Progress includes optional sharing features that let you share your journey with a Fitness Buddy (friend or accountability partner) or with your own registered personal trainer/Coach.

How It Works

  • Disabled by Default: Sharing is turned off by default. Your data stays visible only to you until you choose to enable it.
  • Explicit Permission: You must specifically add a Fitness Buddy, or register under a personal trainer/Coach and expressly grant permission, before anything is shared.
  • Selective Sharing: You choose which photos, measurements, or progress updates to share with a specific buddy or Coach.
  • Revoke Anytime: You can remove a buddy or Coach and stop sharing at any time, and they immediately lose access to your data.
  • Never Public: Shared data is only visible to the specific buddy or Coach you granted access to. It's never made public or shared with anyone else.

Your Control

You have complete control over sharing:

  • Enable or disable sharing at any time
  • Choose individual photos or measurements to share
  • Remove a Fitness Buddy or Coach instantly
  • Delete shared content whenever you want
  • All sharing requires your explicit consent, your data is 100% secured and in your control

Health Data

If you choose to integrate with Apple Health or Google Fit:

  • We only access the specific health data you grant permission for
  • Health data is used solely to populate your measurements and charts
  • You can revoke health data access at any time through your device settings
  • We never share your health data with third parties
  • Health data integration is completely optional

Third-Party Services

Body Progress uses the following trusted services to operate:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, database, and secure cloud storage. Your data is stored in accordance with Google's security standards.
  • Apple Sign In: For secure authentication on iOS devices.
  • Google Sign In: For secure authentication on Android devices.

These services are bound by their own privacy policies and do not have access to use your data beyond providing the technical infrastructure for the app.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your data within the app at any time.
  • Export: Export your photos and measurements data in CSV or image format.
  • Delete: Delete individual photos, measurements, or your entire account.
  • Correction: Edit or update any of your measurements or notes.
  • Portability: Export your data to move to another service if you choose.

Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Your data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Account Deletion: When you delete your account, all your personal data, photos, and measurements are permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Inactive Accounts: We do not delete inactive accounts. Your data remains yours until you choose to delete it.

Children's Privacy

Body Progress is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will delete it immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email. Your continued use of Body Progress after changes are made constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your privacy or this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: bodyProgress@koungasolutions.co.nz
  • Website: koungasolutions.co.nz

We take your privacy seriously and will respond to all requests within 30 days.

Your trust matters: We built Body Progress with privacy at its core. Your transformation journey is personal, and we're committed to keeping it that way.
